How pieces move

  1. You can't move pawns 2 spaces every time. That is only for first move.you have to move once all other times.
  2. You can move bishops diagonally on the same colored squares.
  3. The king can only move one square.
  4. the rook can move horisontal or vertical
  5. The knight can move like an L shape. 3 units up, left, right, down. and one unit to the right or left of it
  6. the queen can move like all peices but the knight.
